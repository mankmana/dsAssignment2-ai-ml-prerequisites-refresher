## Key Takeaways

- Vectors represent quantities with multiple components, while matrices organize and transform collections of values.
- The dot product is important for similarity, projections, matrix multiplication, and many machine-learning calculations.
- Matrix multiplication is different from element-wise multiplication and is used to combine transformations.
- Determinants and inverses help determine whether a system of linear equations has a unique solution.
- Eigenvectors represent important directions of a transformation, while eigenvalues describe how much those directions are scaled.
- PCA uses covariance and eigen-directions to reduce dimensionality while preserving important variation in the data.
- Linear algebra provides the mathematical foundation for many machine-learning and neural-network operations.
